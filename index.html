About Infinch

Infinch connects India's fuel retail outlets — spread across every highway and town — with businesses that need physical space: dark stores, parcel and logistics hubs, EV-charging bays, and small retail kiosks. Every listing on this platform follows the applicable Oil Marketing Company's own commercial-space policy, so pricing is transparent and consistent. Outlet owners monetise space that would otherwise sit idle; businesses get last-mile locations that are already trusted, secure, and easy to reach.
